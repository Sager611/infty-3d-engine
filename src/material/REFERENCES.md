Assets are zlib-compressed, row-major512×512 RGBA8: linear RGB then normalized authored relief. Decode/check bounded1MiB output before mip generation; malformed/checksum/size errors propagate. Each level carries explicit source bytes; shader compilation expands metadata into straight-line calls, so new signed orders need metadata/assets, not runtime loops. Steel0–1 are authored manufacturing variation; Steel2–3 and Brick2–3 use the credited SEM derivatives. Brick1 uses1024-coordinate box(720,168,750.72,198.72) on the8K photo; Brick0 selects separate clean faces with matching mortar borders. Fine clay albedo excludes pore masks; authored cavity relief retains floor variation. Compensated scalar relief is unclipped, RGB bounded. Fine steel relief follows the shader's V brush axis.

Initial tile extents420/42/4.2/0.42mm remain10× apart; finest texels are0.82µm. Coarse layers depict courses/manufacturing variation; fine layers depict porous clay/minerals or abrasion, never tiny bricks. Subpixel structure belongs in filtered roughness, not aliased lines. Mirrored stochastic sampling preserves brush direction and object-local bond; packed identity repeats after256 tiles. Directional diffuse fill and anisotropic studio lobes are artistic lighting approximations, not measured BRDF/environment data. Tone-mapped linear output is sRGB-encoded for an unorm display target. Validate both distant appearance and zoom transitions against the images above; screenshots remain separate from numerical tests.

Scene → Red brick · relief shares Leaf's direct hit approximation. One carrier-height evaluation determines depth; reciprocal incidence fades below |N·ray|=0.05. All bands use object-local points, never camera phase or a second world-position offset. Hardware-filtered RGBA shares phase, carrier mapping and frozen footprint; shifted coordinates drive RGB and per-level, texel-width normal stencils. Fine brick relief gains are8× stronger; mortar and source assets are unchanged. Displacement and shading have no loops, recursion or root search. This favors speed over exact first crossings, silhouettes, occlusion and certified error; steep/close views can distort or miss relief. Carrier picking/cast shadows and the old miss rule remain; no new gap solver.
